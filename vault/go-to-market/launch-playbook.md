# Launch playbook
1. Product Marketing writes the one-line positioning and three proof points.
2. Long-form launch post (600–900 words) with one customer quote and one screenshot.
3. LinkedIn post and newsletter item on launch day; release notes in the app.
4. Landing-page section updated by Design, reviewed by the Head of Design.
5. Nothing is published without the owner's OK.
