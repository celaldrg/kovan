# Customer segments
- **Growth SaaS (50–250 staff)**: product manager is the buyer; wants fast setup and Slack digests.
- **Scale-up SaaS (250–1,000)**: VP Product + Head of Support buy together; need SSO and role permissions.
- Top reasons to buy: one view of customer evidence, fewer "loudest customer wins" decisions.
- Top reasons for churn: no owner after the champion leaves; weak CRM sync.
Support expectations per plan are in [[customers/support-policy]].
