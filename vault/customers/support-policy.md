# Support policy and SLAs
| Severity | Example | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Signal down, data not syncing for all users | 30 min | 4 h |
| P2 | One integration failing, export broken | 2 h | 1 business day |
| P3 | How-to question, minor UI bug | 1 business day | 5 business days |
- Enterprise accounts get named escalation to the Head of Support & Ops.
- Refunds and credits are never promised in a reply; they go to Finance for approval.
- Incident updates every 30 minutes during a P1 until resolved.
Tone follows [[company/writing-style]].
