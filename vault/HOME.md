# Tessellane Labs — home

Tessellane Labs is a 40-person B2B software company. Our product, **Tessellane Signal**, reads a SaaS team's support tickets, sales calls and product usage, and turns them into a ranked list of what customers actually need. This vault is the company's shared memory: every agent reads from it and files its work back into it.
