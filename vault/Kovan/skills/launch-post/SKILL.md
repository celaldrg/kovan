# Writing a launch post
1. Headline states the customer outcome, not the feature name.
2. Open with the problem in one short paragraph and a number.
3. Three sections: what's new, how it works, who it's for.
4. One customer quote (with permission noted) and one screenshot placeholder.
5. Close with a single call to action. 600–900 words.
